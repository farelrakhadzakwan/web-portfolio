Create a restrained, premium visual system.

Possible accent direction:

- deep cobalt blue
- muted dark blue
- subdued burgundy
- dark green

Do NOT use rainbow gradients.

## Typography

Use a strong editorial typographic hierarchy.

Recommended direction:

- primary sans-serif: Geist, Inter, or similar
- optional editorial serif for major display text: Instrument Serif, DM Serif Display, or similar

Typography should do most of the visual work.

Use:

- large but controlled headings
- generous whitespace
- strong contrast between headings and body text
- narrow readable text columns
- clear section hierarchy

Avoid oversized headings that occupy the entire viewport without meaningful information.

# 10. PROJECT CASE STUDY DESIGN

Each major project should have its own case-study page.

Do NOT make case studies into generic marketing pages.

Each case study should answer:

1. Context
2. Problem
3. Constraints
4. My role
5. Approach
6. Key technical decisions
7. Experiments
8. Results
9. Limitations
10. What I learned

The key principle:

> Show judgment, not just output.

Include trade-offs where factual.

For example:

### Why GMM-HMM?

Explain why the project used probabilistic latent-state modeling instead of simply presenting the model name.

### Why 20 Hz vs 40 Hz?

Explain the sampling-rate comparison and its implications.

### What were the limitations?

Explicitly acknowledge dataset limitations and methodological constraints.

This makes the case study more credible.

Do not turn every section into promotional copy.
