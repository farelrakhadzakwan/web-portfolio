01 / RESEARCH

Latent EEG Representation Using GMM-HMM

Research · EEG · Probabilistic Modeling

EEG Bandpower & Latent State Visualization
Role AI Researcher
Timeline Aug 2025 – Jul 2026
Tools Python, NumPy, SciPy, Scikit-learn, hmmlearn
Context

Understanding the temporal dynamics of brain activity requires modeling both the structural features of EEG signals and their transitions over time. This research focuses on analyzing latent structures in prefrontal cortex signals from 50 participants.

Approach & Technical Decisions

Why GMM-HMM? Instead of mapping signals directly to a classification label, we used a Gaussian Mixture Model - Hidden Markov Model to study the probabilistic transitions between latent states. This provides interpretability into how activities induce distinct, persistent neurological states rather than just predicting a label.

We extracted Welch PSD bandpower features and compared representations across different frequency resolutions (20 Hz vs 40 Hz). The 20 Hz resolution yielded a more robust cluster separation (Silhouette score 0.3601), while the 40 Hz model optimized the Bayesian Information Criterion (BIC).

Limitations & Reflection

The model identifies distinct latent states, but interpreting these purely as physiological stress requires careful clinical validation. The current pipeline establishes a robust mathematical representation of the signal dynamics, laying the groundwork for real-time monitoring applications.
